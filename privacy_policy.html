Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Introduction

NIRdroid ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

Information We Collect

NIRdroid is a security toolkit that operates primarily offline. We collect the minimum information necessary to provide our services:

  • Network Information: When you use network tools (ping, traceroute, port scanning), we process the IP addresses and domains you specify. This data is processed locally on your device.
  • API Data: When you use OSINT, IP geolocation, or other online features, we may send your specified target (domain, IP, phone number) to third-party APIs you configure. No data is stored by us.
  • Device Information: We may collect anonymous crash reports and device model information to improve app stability.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the security and network analysis services you request
  • To improve app functionality and user experience
  • To diagnose and fix technical issues

Third-Party Services

NIRdroid may integrate with third-party APIs that you explicitly configure:

  • IP Geolocation services (ip-api.com, etc.)
  • Phone number lookup services (numverify, abstractapi, veriphone)
  • Certificate transparency (crt.sh)
  • Have I Been Pwned (HIBP)
  • Shodan, CVE services, and others you choose to enable

Each of these services has its own privacy policy. You control which APIs to enable and what data to send.

Data Storage

All API keys are stored locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. We do not transmit or store your API keys on our servers. No personal data is collected or stored by us.

Permissions

The App requires the following permissions:

  • Internet: To perform network scans and API lookups
  • Location (Wi-Fi): To scan nearby Wi-Fi networks
  • Vibrate: For user feedback

Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of changes by updating the "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

Email: privacy@nirdroid.app

Data Deletion

All app data is stored locally. To delete your data, uninstall the app or clear app data via Android Settings > Apps > NIRdroid > Storage > Clear Data.
